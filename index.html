Tempo

Locks in 4–8 seconds on music with a clear beat. If it reads half or double what you feel, use ÷2 or ×2; that ambiguity is inherent to tempo, not a fault. Reset when the song changes. The four dots follow the beat; they mark beats, not where the bar starts.

Tuner

Pick your instrument and tuning. The tuner follows the nearest string; if a string is far off, select it so the tuner aims at that string instead. In tune means within ±3 cents.

Loudness

K-weighted per ITU-R BS.1770: S is short-term (3 s), M momentary (400 ms), I integrated since start or reset. A mono input is metered as one channel; meters that treat mono as dual-mono read 3 dB higher. Microphone readings depend on your gain and are relative, not calibrated.

Privacy

All analysis runs in this page. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.
